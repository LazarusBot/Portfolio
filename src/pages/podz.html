Podz

Building a modern iOS app from scratch to find and book mountain shelters, the pods. I designed it end to end: the brand, the mascots, the design system, every screen and the motion.

Role
Product designer
Platform
iOS
Scope
Brand, UI, motion

Logo

A pod seen from the side: a small shelter with a sloped roof. It works alone as a symbol and next to the wordmark, on four colours taken from the app.

App icon

The pod becomes a glass block that sits above its shadow. It ships in the six iOS modes. Pick one to preview it.

Imagery

Real shelters from the Alps and the Dolomites carry the brand: the wordmark sits on the photo, nothing else.

Characters

Hexi, Podzi and Roundi live in the empty states, the loaders and the friend lists. Each one is drawn from the logo's simple shapes.

The app

From the first launch to the booking: onboarding, sign-in with or without an account, a feed of pods, Lenny the search assistant, the inbox and the profile. Scroll or drag through the screens.

Pod details

Seven takes on the same pod page, from a classic sheet to a full-bleed photo, to find the right balance between the place, the availability and the booking button.

Components

Location cards and stacks, filters, counters, the tab bar, the selection bar and a payment button that goes from Apple Pay to done. Each component has its states, ready for prototyping.

Pinned

Friends pin the pods they like. The card grows with the photos added by the group.

Member badge

Each member gets a badge, used in the app and on the motion pieces.
