Real app, in beta
Role
Product designer
Platform
iOS
Scope
Brand, UI, motion

Description

Building from scratch a modern iOS app to find and book mountain shelters, the pods. Brand, characters, design system, screens and motion.

Logo evolution

From a flat red tile to a glass pod: five steps to give the shelter more volume and character.

App icon

The final icon in the six iOS modes. Move over it, then pick a mode.

Imagery

Real shelters from the Alps and the Dolomites carry the brand.

Characters

Hexi, Podzi and Roundi are built from the same shapes as the logo. They keep an eye on you, and they get bored if you don't move.

The app

Onboarding, sign-in, a feed of pods, Lenny the search assistant, the inbox and the profile. Drag through the screens.

Pinned

Friends pin the pods they like. The card grows with the photos added by the group.

Member badge

Each member gets a badge on their profile. The strap gets a light tug and the badge settles back on its own.
